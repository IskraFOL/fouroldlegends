FOUR OLD LEGENDS / FOUR-OLD-LEGENDS

Four Old Legends is an atmospheric Minecraft horror mod about four legends sharing one world: Herobrine, NULL, Entity 303 and Lick.

Created by BrainMystycal.
Follow traces of their presence, explore strange places and discover recordings.
Check the chosen file page for supported game versions, dependencies and changes.
